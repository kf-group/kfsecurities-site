This policy explains how KF Securities Private Limited ("KF Securities", "we", "us") collects and uses personal information when you use kfsecurities.com or contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, mobile number, email, business name and city.
  • Information and documents you share for an engagement, such as PAN, GSTIN, incorporation documents, financial statements and identity documents.
  • Payment references such as UPI transaction IDs or Razorpay payment IDs. We do not see or store your card or bank details.
  • Basic website usage data, such as pages visited and browser type.

How we use it

  • To prepare quotes and deliver the services you request, including filings with government portals.
  • To contact you about your engagement, including on WhatsApp.
  • To meet legal, tax, accounting and KYC obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed with government authorities and portals for your filings, our partner advocates and chartered accountants working on your matter, payment providers, our partner stockbroker and fund houses for investment services, KF Group companies that support our operations, and authorities where the law requires it.

Storage and security

We store documents securely, limit access to the people working on your matter, and keep information only as long as needed or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by writing to social@kfsecurities.com.
